=======
Credits
=======

EasyHPC started in 2022 as the shared documentation of an undergraduate research course at Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez, Santiago, Chile. The students wrote the pages while learning each topic, so that the next ones could learn it faster.

The engineering problems in :doc:`nn` follow the course by Professor María Thomsen Solís at Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez.
